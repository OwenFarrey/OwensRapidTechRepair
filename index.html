About Me

Hi, I'm Owen. I'm an IT student at Grand Valley State University, where I've made the Dean's List.

I've been taking things apart for as long as I can remember, my parents' phones, old computers, anything electronic. That curiosity led me to an internship at a local phone repair shop during my freshman year. There, I learned how to diagnose issues, source quality parts, and repair everything from cracked screens to faulty charge ports.

After a year of training, I realized I could do more by striking out on my own. So I launched Owen's Rapid Tech Repair a mobile service that brings honest, affordable repairs directly to you.

I use quality parts (new, refurbished, or aftermarket), explain every step, and charge fair prices.

Service area: Grand Rapids, Allendale, GVSU campus, Walker, Standale, Grandville, and within a 20‑minute drive. I'm available Monday–Sunday.
